About Me

I am a Technical UI/UX Engineer and Product Designer who bridges the gap between human-centric design and high-performance engineering. My approach is rooted in a deep understanding of the full product lifecycle: I lead user discovery sessions to translate complex needs into strategic roadmaps, then execute the entire UX stack—from Information Architecture and User Research to Wireframing and High-Fidelity Prototyping in Figma.

What sets me apart is my ability to speak the language of both users and machines. With a Master of Computer Applications and experience in Angular environments, I don't just hand off designs; I build them. I specialize in "Vibe Coding"—leveraging cutting-edge AI tools like Cursor and Antigravity to streamline frontend development, optimize codebases, and ensure that the final product is a pixel-perfect reflection of the initial vision. Whether I’m conducting a stakeholder meeting or refactoring CSS, my goal is to create seamless, intuitive digital experiences that drive real engagement.
